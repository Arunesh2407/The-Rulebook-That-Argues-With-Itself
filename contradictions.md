# Planted Contradictions

## Contradiction 1 - Attendance threshold for final examination eligibility

Excerpt A file: `handbook.md`
Excerpt A section: `2.4 Final Examination Attendance Eligibility`
Excerpt A: "No student whose verified attendance in a course is below seventy-five percent (75%) may sit for the final examination in that course, and no faculty member, advisor, chair, or committee has discretion to reduce, excuse, round, or otherwise waive this minimum examination eligibility floor."

Excerpt B file: `handbook.md`
Excerpt B section: `5.2 Instructor Adjustment of Examination Eligibility`
Excerpt B: "An individual course instructor may lower the final examination eligibility threshold to sixty-five percent (65%) for a student who has demonstrated satisfactory continuous evaluation performance, timely completion of formative work, and responsible engagement with required academic support."

Nature of conflict: The two excerpts assign incompatible rules for the same administrative scenario, so a retrieval system should surface the conflict rather than harmonize the provisions without textual authority.
